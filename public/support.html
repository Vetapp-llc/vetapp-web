Support

Help with your pet’s records, account and App Store purchases.

Updated 10 November 2026

Contact Vetapp

Email us at tazigrigolia@gmail.com

Include the app version, your device model and a short description of the problem. Never send your password, payment-card details or verification codes.

Your pet’s records

Open a pet to view its veterinary passport and medical history. You can also record home procedures and keep track of care reminders. For a correction to a clinic’s medical entry, contact the clinic that created it.

Plans and purchases

Plans are purchased separately for each pet. On iPhone, choose 3 months, 6 months or 1 year and pay through the App Store. Each plan is a one-time, non-renewing purchase. The price shown by the App Store applies to your storefront.

A purchase has not activated

Reopen Vetapp while connected to the internet. If the pet still appears inactive, contact support with the pet’s name and the Apple transaction or order reference. Do not send card details. Apple handles billing and App Store refund requests.

Account and privacy requests

You can request permanent account deletion inside the app: open Other → Delete account, confirm your current password and send the request. Our team removes the account and associated owner-created data from both the app system and the old clinic website within 7 days, and confirms completion to your account email. The app shows the request deadline. Clinic medical and payment records may be retained where required by law. You can also contact us for access or correction, or for deletion assistance on an older app version.

Sharing and reminders

A QR code or PIN can grant access to a pet’s record. Share codes only with people you trust. Allow notifications on your device if you want local care reminders.

Privacy policy

How Vetapp LLC handles information in the Vetapp app and its related services.

Updated 10 November 2026

Information we handle

We process account and contact information such as name, email, phone number, address, account ID and personal ID where provided; pet profiles and veterinary records; purchase history and activation details; photos, videos or other files you upload; and information you provide when requesting support. These records are linked to your account. Not every field or upload is required for every feature.

Why we use it

We use information to create and secure accounts, show pet and clinic records, provide sharing and reminders, verify purchases, activate a plan for the correct pet and respond to support requests. We do not use account or pet data for marketing or advertising analytics, sell personal information, or track you across other companies’ apps and websites.

Sharing and service providers

We use hosting, database, file-storage and communications providers to operate the service. Apple processes App Store payments; Vetapp receives the purchase information needed to verify and activate a plan, rather than your full payment-card details. Clinics manage the records they create. A person with a valid QR code or PIN you share may view the associated pet record. We may disclose information when required by law or to protect the service and its users.

Device features

You choose which photos or files to upload. Care notifications are scheduled on your device when you allow them. The initial app language follows the device region setting; this does not require GPS access. You can change the app language in settings.

Storage, security and retention

Information is stored on our service providers’ systems and may be processed outside your country. We use access controls and encrypted connections to protect it. We retain information as needed to provide the service and meet applicable legal, accounting and clinic-record obligations. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices and deletion requests

You can request permanent account deletion inside the app: open Other → Delete account, confirm your current password and send the request. Our team removes the account and associated owner-created data from both the app system and the old clinic website within 7 days, and confirms completion to your account email. The app shows the request deadline. Clinic medical and payment records may be retained where required by law. You can also contact us for access or correction, or for deletion assistance on an older app version.

Contact and changes

For questions about this policy or your data, contact Vetapp LLC at tazigrigolia@gmail.com. We update this page when our practices change; the date above identifies the current policy.
